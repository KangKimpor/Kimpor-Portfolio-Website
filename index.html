About

I'm a junior Civil Engineering student at Paragon International University, Phnom Penh, on a fully funded national scholarship. Alongside my degree, I work with Singbuild Construction Co., Ltd., a design & build contractor established in 2015, coordinating hotel, residential, retail, F&B and office fit-out projects.

My daily work is the paperwork that keeps a site moving: progress reports, quantity takeoff (QTO) and BOQ documentation, procurement and delivery tracking, RFIs and transmittals, method statements, defect lists and handover packages. I also visit site to verify that what is reported matches what is built.

I enjoy building small tools that remove repetitive work, and I'm steadily connecting what I learn in the classroom with what actually happens on site.
