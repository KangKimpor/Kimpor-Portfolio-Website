01 · About

Engineering student with real site mileage

I'm a junior Civil Engineering student at Paragon International University, Phnom Penh, studying on a fully funded scholarship awarded through a nationwide examination. Alongside my degree, I work with Singbuild Construction Co., Ltd., a Phnom Penh design & build contractor established in 2015, supporting project coordination across hotel, residential, retail, F&B and office fit-out projects.

My day-to-day covers the paperwork that keeps a site moving: daily, weekly and monthly progress reports, quantity takeoff and BOQ documentation, procurement and delivery tracking, RFIs and transmittals, method statements, defect lists, and handover packages, plus site visits to verify that what's reported matches what's built.

I enjoy building small tools that remove repetitive work, and I'm steadily connecting what I learn in the classroom with what actually happens on site.
